Mehdi's Association for Political (Reform)* (MAP)

Commonly known as MAP · PAM, but improved

Not to be confused with PAM, a map, or a map of PAM.

Unlike PAM (the Authenticity and Modernity Party), MAP (Mehdi's Association for Political (Reform)), commonly known as MAP, is a renowned political party which was founded in 2026 after a typing error in a PAM document was accepted by the Ministry of the Interior.

MAP is PAM's self-declared successor and describes the relationship as "a merger in progress." It has one member, (and looking for more), one desk (probably), and no seats in Parliament, which they claim is their a delibrate intentional lean operation.

Etymology

MAP takes its letters from Mehdi's, Association, and Political. The R in Reform is not represented; the party keeps it in a drawer, available for inspection by appointment.
